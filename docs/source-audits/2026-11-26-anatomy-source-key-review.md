# Anatomy MCQ source-key review — 26 November 2026

## Scope

This note records answer-key and wording problems identified while reviewing the extractable text of the supplied Head and Neck MCQs, Upper Limb MCQs and *MCQs in Anatomy: Thorax, Abdomen, Pelvis and Perineum*, Volume 2 (2013). It is a quality-control note, not a transcription. Copyrighted question stems and option sets are not reproduced here.

## Head and Neck source

| Item | Issue found | Correct treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | The keyed answer treats all listed nerves, including facial nerve via the tympanic plexus, as sensory supply to the auricle. | The usual cutaneous supply is from auriculotemporal (V3), great auricular (C2–C3), lesser occipital (C2) and auricular branch of vagus (X). The tympanic plexus supplies middle-ear mucosa, not the auricular skin. Avoid “all of the above”. |
| 11 | The heading labels the abducent nerve as “CN V1”, conflating CN VI with the ophthalmic division of CN V. | Correct the nerve identity before assessing its course. CN VI and V1 are distinct nerves; both enter the orbit through the superior orbital fissure but have different functions and relationships. |
| 15 | More than one listed structure is outside the carotid sheath. | The internal and common carotid arteries, internal jugular vein and vagus nerve are sheath contents. Glossopharyngeal and hypoglossal nerves are not sheath contents; external jugular vein is also outside it. The stem/key is not a valid single-best-answer item as written. |
| 22 | The listed options do not correctly describe the ophthalmic artery's route. | The ophthalmic artery enters the orbit through the optic canal with the optic nerve, not through the superior orbital fissure. Rebuild the item rather than retaining the supplied key. |
| 25 | Several statements are anatomically incorrect, including the description of ligamenta flava attachment and the lumbar transverse foramina; the intervertebral disc is also misclassified if described as a primary cartilaginous joint. | Ligamenta flava connect adjacent laminae; transverse foramina are characteristic of cervical vertebrae; intervertebral discs form secondary cartilaginous symphyses. The original single-best-answer framing is invalid. |

## Upper Limb source

| Item | Issue found | Correct treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1, Q17 | More than one brachial-plexus statement is incorrect: lateral-cord root values are overstated, and the suprascapular nerve is not solely a C5 branch. | The lateral cord is usually C5–C7; the suprascapular nerve arises from the upper trunk, usually C5–C6. The item needs a revised option set. |
| Section 1, Q18 | No listed root-value pairing is fully satisfactory as a single correct answer. | Use complete, standard root values and allow for common anatomical variation only where explicitly stated. |
| Section 1, Q26 | It suggests a midshaft humeral fracture may paralyse the whole triceps. | The radial nerve's branches to the triceps arise proximal to or around the radial groove; a typical midshaft radial-groove injury more classically weakens wrist/finger extension. Avoid claiming complete triceps paralysis without specifying lesion level. |
| Section 2, Q15 | Several options are false, not just one: axillary nerve root values are overstated, and the axillary nerve does not pass below teres major. | The axillary nerve usually carries C5–C6 fibres and passes through the quadrangular space above teres major, accompanying the posterior circumflex humeral vessels. |
| Section 2, Q25 | The stated number of divisions is wrong and several branch-count statements are not consistent with the standard plexus pattern. | The brachial plexus has five roots, three trunks, six divisions and three cords. Rebuild the question using the standard organisation rather than retaining a single-answer key. |

### Additional Pelvis and Perineum findings

| Item | Issue found | Correct treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Q127, statement D | The explanation says contraction of puborectalis helps defaecation. This reverses its principal continence role. | Puborectalis contraction maintains the anorectal angle and supports continence. Defaecation requires relaxation of the puborectalis sling, with straightening of the anorectal angle. |
| Q131, statement E | The explanation presents portal hypertension as the mechanism producing internal haemorrhoids through the portosystemic anastomosis. | The superior and middle/inferior rectal venous channels do form a portosystemic connection, but internal haemorrhoids are principally enlargement/displacement of the normal anal vascular cushions; portal hypertension should not be presented as their general or defining cause. Distinguish rectal varices from haemorrhoids. |

| Q133, statement B | The explanation suggests the female urethra may lengthen from about 4 cm to 10 cm in later pregnancy. | The female urethra is generally about 4 cm long. The stated pregnancy-related increase to 10 cm is not a standard anatomical fact and should not be taught without a reliable, specific source. |
| Q135, statement E | The explanation calls “secondary spermatogonia” haploid. | Spermatogonia are diploid. Meiosis produces haploid secondary spermatocytes; spermatids and spermatozoa are also haploid. “Secondary spermatogonia” is incorrect terminology. |
| Q139, statement A | The explanation assigns the seminal vesicles and ejaculatory ducts to the pelvic part of the urogenital sinus. | The seminal vesicles and ejaculatory ducts develop from the mesonephric (Wolffian) ducts. The prostate and much of the urethral epithelium arise from the urogenital sinus. Do not attribute the seminal vesicles/ejaculatory ducts to the urogenital sinus. |

These are targeted findings from the answer/explanation section, not evidence that every item in the volume has been checked. The source's true/false format is retained only as context; no source question text is reproduced.

## DxDrill policy and follow-up

- Source answer-key errors are not imported into DxDrill.
- New DxDrill questions are independently authored and carry their own explanation.
- When a source item has multiple false statements, an incomplete key or an incorrect premise, it is not converted into a single-best-answer item without rebuilding the question.
- This note is a targeted audit of identified issues, not expert sign-off of every source item or the complete question bank.
