# Anatomy: FirsTest — source coverage review (26 November 2026)

## Source and handling

- Source: *Anatomy: FirsTest*, Dr Suyog Santosh Moon, first edition, Kalam Books, 2007.
- Length: 336 scanned pages.
- OCR: all 336 page images were processed; the resulting text is available for review in the working session.
- Copyright handling: no question stems, option sets, explanations or answer keys from the book have been copied into DxDrill. The scan is used to identify subject coverage and concepts for independently authored questions.

## Chapter structure identified from the contents

The source is organised into:
1. Head and Neck
2. Neuroanatomy
3. Abdomen
4. Lower limb
5. Thorax
6. Histology
7. Joints
8. Embryology
9. Upper Limb
10. Miscellaneous

The question-and-explanation portions include gross anatomy, neuroanatomy, histology, embryology, applied anatomy and clinical correlations. OCR quality varies by page, especially for diagrams, columns and low-contrast print; OCR text is therefore a navigation aid, not a substitute for checking the page image where a detail is uncertain.

## Scope limits

This is a complete OCR pass and a structural check of the listed DxDrill banks, not a full expert validation of every answer in the 336-page source or every existing question in the banks. OCR output requires visual verification when wording, diagrams, labels or answer choices are unclear. Additional topic-gap work and content-level answer checks remain appropriate, particularly for histology, embryology, joints and the miscellaneous section.
