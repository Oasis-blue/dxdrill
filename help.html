Trouble loading DxDrill?

Tap the button. It takes about 20 seconds and checks whether your phone can reach DxDrill. It does not sign you in, and it does not change anything.

What I found

    Things that fix most problems

    1. Switch networks. If you are on Wi-Fi, try mobile data, or the other way round. Some networks reach DxDrill's servers badly, even when other sites work.
    2. Change your phone's DNS to 1.1.1.1. On Android: Settings → Network → Private DNS → one.one.one.one. On iPhone: install the free "1.1.1.1" app from Cloudflare and switch it on.
    3. Try another browser app, for example Chrome or Firefox.
    4. Clear DxDrill's saved copy with the button below, then open DxDrill again.
    5. Wait a few minutes and try again. Your scores are saved on your phone, so nothing is lost.
